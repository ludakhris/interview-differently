# Security policy

Learn Differently and Interview Differently (learndifferently.tech, interviewdifferently.com and their APIs).

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately, by either:

- emailing security@learndifferently.tech, or
- opening a private report at https://github.com/ludakhris/interview-differently/security/advisories/new.

Please do not open a public issue. Include what you found, where, and the steps to reproduce it. We will confirm we
received your report within three business days and keep you informed until it is fixed.

Test only with your own accounts and fictional data. Do not access or change other people's data, degrade the service,
or run automated scans that send large volumes of traffic.

The same contacts are published at `/.well-known/security.txt` on each site. Its `Expires` date is renewed each year.
